Frequently Considered.

How does AuraRing function with zero subscription fees?

We treat biometric hardware like high horology or fine cameras: you purchase the instrument, and the insights are yours forever. We do not maintain server paywalls or hostage fees. Our business model relies on hardware excellence, not recurring monthly subscription fees.

Is the companion app compatible with iOS and Android?

Yes. The Aura companion software runs seamlessly on iPhone (iOS 15 and above) and modern Android devices. It natively syncs with Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava, and TrainingPeaks.

Can I wear the ring in hot saunas, ocean water, or heavy lifting?

AuraRing is forged from solid Grade-5 titanium with a sealed medical resin inner core rated to 50 meters (10 ATM). It withstands ocean saltwater, high-heat dry saunas, and chlorinated pools without degradation.

What occurs if my ring does not fit comfortably?

Every purchase includes a 30-Day Risk-Free Trial from the day your titanium ring arrives. If the fit feels imperfect, we provide one complimentary size exchange with prepaid return shipping.

How fast will my sizing kit and titanium ring arrive?

Your complimentary Precision Sizing Set is dispatched within 24 hours of ordering (arriving in 2–3 business days via priority airmail). Once you confirm your size in the app, your custom titanium AuraRing is calibrated and delivered within 48–72 hours via express courier. Total time from order to finger is under 7 business days.

What is the lifespan of the battery over 2–5 years?

AuraRing uses solid-state high-density microcells rated for 400+ complete charge cycles with less than 15% degradation (equivalent to over 5 years of daily continuous use). Should you experience any abnormal battery decline within your first 2 years, we replace the entire ring free under warranty.

Which finger is recommended for the most accurate readings?

We recommend wearing AuraRing on your index finger for the highest optical PPG signal accuracy from the palmar digital arteries. However, the middle and ring fingers provide equally valid sleep staging. Our gentle internal chamfer naturally accommodates minor seasonal temperature swelling.
