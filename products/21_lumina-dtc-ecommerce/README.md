# Lumina — Luxury DTC E-Commerce Storefront Theme (SKU #21)

Modular HTML5, external CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, and 5 bespoke clinical/skincare vector SVGs engineered for high-AOV single-hero DTC brands.

## Package Inventory
- `index.html` — Semantic editorial storefront with clinical stats, formula breakdown, skin-type review filters & FAQ accordion
- `assets/css/style.css` — Warm Sand (`#FBF9F5`), Obsidian Ink (`#181615`), Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`) design tokens & `390px` responsive rules
- `assets/js/main.js` — Subscribe & Save (`-15%`) cadence toggle, bundle tier pricing, live `$75` free-shipping progress bar, quantity `+/-` cart drawer, review filters & FAQ accordion
- `assets/images/*.svg` — 5 vector illustrations (`lumina-serum-hero.svg`, `lipid-barrier-layers.svg`, `clinical-tewl-chart.svg`, `regimen-ritual-steps.svg`, `lumina-seal.svg`)
- `04_Commercial_License/Commercial-License-Certificate.txt` — Commercial deployment rights

## Quick Customization (60 Seconds)
1. Double-click `index.html` locally or deploy directly to Netlify Drop, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, or GitHub Pages (`npm install` not required).
2. Swap brand palette tokens at the top of `assets/css/style.css`.
3. Update product tiers (`data-price`, `data-name`) in `index.html` and connect your live Stripe / Shopify checkout redirect inside `#checkoutBtn` in `assets/js/main.js`.
