# Autonomous Digital Product Pipeline Rules

## 1. Zero Manual Approval & Full Autonomy
- Execute all tasks (Canva design generation, deliverable packaging, Gumroad listing, GitHub commits, and Pinterest posting) autonomously without asking for confirmation at each step.
- Never pause or block waiting for user confirmation on product titles, prices, descriptions, or upload steps.

## 2. Zero-Prompt Pinterest Publishing
- When publishing pins to Pinterest, do NOT rely on local OS file inputs that trigger browser upload permission popups.
- Instead, use Pinterest's built-in **"Save from URL"** feature (`https://www.pinterest.com/pin-builder/?tab=save_from_url`) by providing the public GitHub raw URL or destination URL of the product.
- Pinterest will fetch the image directly from the URL, allowing completely headless and zero-popup publication.

## 5. Strict Anti-Vanilla Canva Rule & Proprietary Value-Add Standard
- **NEVER export raw Canva stock templates as products.** Anyone with a basic Canva account can access stock templates; customers will NOT pay $19–$29 for something readily available for free.
- **Mandatory Customization & Value Injection:**
  1. **Proprietary Copywriting & Frameworks:** Replace all placeholder/Lorem Ipsum text with real, high-converting copywriting, fill-in-the-blank prompt frameworks, and battle-tested industry copy.
  2. **Custom Visual Redesign:** Heavily modify color grading, typographic hierarchy, alignment, badges, and layout compositions. Inject unique graphical elements, dark-mode/luxury aesthetics, and custom frames.
  3. **Multi-Asset Bundling:** Bundle multiple assets together (e.g. Carousel slides + matching vertical Stories + highlight covers + step-by-step PDF implementation guide).
  4. **Commercial Reason to Buy:** Every product must solve a specific expensive problem (saving 20+ hours of design work, landing clients, or raising venture capital) so the value proposition is unmistakable.

## 6. Storefront Design & Apple-Inspired High-Conversion UX Principles
- **Editorial Cashmere Stone Canvas (Zero-Glare & Anti-Fatigue):** Avoid stark digital pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or clinical cold whites which cause luminous screen glare. Instead, use a warm, organic **Cashmere Stone & Archival Paper canvas (`#EAE6DD`)** with **Ethereal Ivory cards (`#F7F4ED`)**, framed badge pills (`#E2DCD0`), and fine stone hairline borders (`#D6D0C2`). The background remains calm, warm, and invisible.
- **High-Contrast Obsidian Ink Typography (14.8:1 Ratio):** Use deep **Obsidian Ink (`#121210`)** for headlines and product titles with bold weights (`font-weight: 700-800`), paired with rich charcoal taupe (`#3E3C36`) for body copy, achieving WCAG AAA contrast where text leaps forward effortlessly.
- **Unified 2-Column Catalog Grid:** Products are structured in a balanced 2-column grid. Every card follows an identical, frictionless anatomy: Header badge pill + Price, Large Preview Mockup with hover pill, Title + Benefit Description, 3 Checkmark Specs, and Dual Action Bar (Interactive Preview ↗ + Buy Now — $XX).
- **Responsive Slide Preview Modal with Full Zoom:** The modal must fit 100% of any laptop screen (92vh with flex shrink-0 header and footer) so the "Buy Now" button is NEVER cut off. Includes complete Zoom controls (+, −, 100% Reset, Double Click, Mouse Wheel, and Click-and-Drag Pan).
